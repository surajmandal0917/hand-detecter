    HAND FRAME

    Hand-tracked AR. A 3D object lives inside a frame you hold in the air.

    1. Make an L shape with one hand (thumb and index out, other fingers curled). A 3D frame appears with an object inside.
    2. Show your other hand open: the object breaks into particles.
    3. Close it to a fist: the particles rebuild as the next object.
